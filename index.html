About us

An established omnichannel retailer.

ECOHAVEN GROUP LLC is an omnichannel retailer selling consumer brands in the United States and Latin America.

We work with authorized U.S. distributors and brands and sell directly to consumers through our stores, our own online store and Amazon.

Our group has operated in Panama since 2016 with three physical stores through BYGGA Retail Group. We also maintain operations and offices in Bogotá, Colombia, home to our Business Structuring & Development Office.
